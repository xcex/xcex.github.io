Mission Statement

Our cities are becoming more sterile and indifferent. Change is necessary.


It is clear that modernist architecture and urban planning has largely failed to create livable, meaningful and sustainable places that people actually want to live in. Instead, modern building practices are unsustainable and harmful for our mental and physical health, as shown in many scientific works in the last decades.
Solutions to many urban problems can be found in the wealth of knowledge accumulated over the past centuries. But ''classical'' architecture is often not taught in most architecture schools, not even as optional courses. Architecture students wanting to learn classical architecture are often isolated and don't know that people which share their interest exist close by.
This is why we created the Acanthus Network. We act as mediators to bring together individual architecture students locally and help them start local study groups for classical architecture. Our long term goal is to help architecture students to advocate for change in their local university curricula to start teaching classical architecture again.
